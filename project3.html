This is a project for my favorite singer Zhou Shen. He performs five different characters in a fairytale. A little girl, the narrator, the young warrior, the dragon and the king (not counting the baby and the princess who didn't sing). The song is about: There was a dragon who brought disaster to the kingdom, kidnapping the princess and disappearing. The people were distressed and a brave warrior volunteered to rescue the princess.
